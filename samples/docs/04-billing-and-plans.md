# Billing and plans

## Plans

- **Starter** — free, up to 1 million events per month, 30 days of dashboard history, community support.
- **Growth** — $499 per month, up to 50 million events per month, 13 months of history, email support with a one-business-day response.
- **Enterprise** — custom pricing, volume discounts, SSO, a dedicated support engineer and a 99.9% uptime SLA.

## Overage

On Growth, events above the monthly allowance are billed at $8 per additional million. We never drop events because you went over your allowance; we email account owners at 80% and 100% of the allowance.

## Invoices and payment

Invoices are issued on the first day of each month and are due within 30 days. Growth customers can pay by card; Enterprise customers can also pay by bank transfer. Download past invoices from **Settings → Billing**.

## Refunds and cancellation

You can cancel at any time from the Billing page; the plan stays active until the end of the paid month. We refund annual Growth plans pro rata if you cancel within the first 60 days. Monthly plans are not refunded.
